Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

At SkillKit, we believe data privacy is a fundamental right. This Privacy Policy details our strict on-device data practices and principles.

1. Zero Data Collection

SkillKit is designed from the ground up to respect your privacy. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or rent any of your personal information, activity, or content. No user accounts or registrations are required to use the application.

2. On-Device Storage and Processing

SkillKit inspects local SKILL.md and agent definition files on your Mac filesystem. The entire regex linter and security rule engine runs offline in local processes; zero code or prompts are sent over the network.

3. Third-Party Analytics and Ads

SkillKit contains zero third-party advertising frameworks, tracking cookies, or commercial analytics SDKs. We do not track your activity across other applications or websites.

4. Children's Privacy

Our application does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). The app operates without remote server logging or user identity tracking.

5. Security

Because your data remains strictly on your device, its security is guaranteed by Apple's built-in operating system encryption and application sandboxing.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
