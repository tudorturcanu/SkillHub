Frequently Asked Questions

What does SkillKit check for?

SkillKit scans SKILL.md files for prompt injection vulnerabilities, unvalidated tool manifests, excessive permissions, and frontmatter formatting errors.

Does SkillKit upload my custom instructions?

No. All security audits execute 100% locally on your Mac.

What is the scoring metric?

SkillKit scores files from 0 to 100 based on the presence and severity of flagged patterns.
